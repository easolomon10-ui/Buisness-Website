# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

High school soccer players (primarily) and their parents, trying to get noticed by college coaches during recruiting. Secondary audience: the college coaches themselves, who land on the site via a shared link and need to evaluate a player fast. Context: players are competing against hundreds of cold emails and generic highlight-reel links coaches receive every season; the job to be done is standing out and looking credible on first impression.

## Product Purpose

Athletefolio sells custom recruitment websites for high school soccer players, built by Eileen Solomon (a former Boston University soccer player), delivered in 5-7 days. The site itself is the sales pitch: it must demonstrate the exact quality and professionalism a client's own recruitment site will have. Success looks like a visitor (player or parent) filling out the "Get Started" contact form.

## Brand Personality

Confident, driven, competitive, professional. Athletic edge balanced with credibility an adult (coach or parent) will trust — not a gimmicky sports-fan site, and not a generic corporate agency either. Voice comes from lived experience: built by a player who went through recruiting twice and knows the process firsthand.

## Anti-references

No specific sites named. Avoid: generic templated "website builder" aesthetics, stock corporate-agency polish that erases the founder's personal story, and overly casual/gimmicky sports-fan design that would undercut credibility with coaches and parents.

## Design Principles

- **Credibility first**: the founder's story and specific results should feel earned and personal, never stock or generic.
- **Coach's-eye clarity**: information architecture optimized for a busy recruiter scanning fast — stats, highlights, and contact info surface immediately, no fluff in the way.
- **Competitive edge without gimmick**: bold and athletic, but professional enough that a coach or parent takes it seriously.
- **One clear conversion path**: every section should funnel toward the "Get Started" contact form.
- **Built by an athlete, for athletes**: peer credibility (a college player selling to aspiring college players) should read through in tone and specifics, not just be stated once in the About section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: sufficient color contrast (body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1), full keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text on photos, accessible form labels and error states.
