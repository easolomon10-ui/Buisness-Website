# Design Context

See [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) for full detail. Summary:

- **Register**: brand (marketing/landing page — design IS the product)
- **What this is**: Athletefolio's own marketing site, selling custom recruitment websites to high school soccer players.

**Design principles:**
- Credibility first — the founder's story and results should feel earned, not stock.
- Coach's-eye clarity — busy recruiters should find stats, highlights, and contact info immediately.
- Competitive edge without gimmick — bold and athletic, but professional enough for coaches and parents.
- One clear conversion path — every section funnels toward the "Get Started" contact form.
- Built by an athlete, for athletes — peer credibility should read through in tone, not just be stated once.
