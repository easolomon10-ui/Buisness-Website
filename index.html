About the Founder

Built by a player who's been through it.

I'm Eileen Solomon, a former college soccer player who knows exactly how hard the recruiting process is. During my undergraduate career, I competed as a student athlete at Boston University for four years. Following a medical redshirt season, I utilized my remaining year of eligibility to compete while pursuing graduate studies at the University of Iowa. Having gone through college recruitment twice, I've learned a thing or two. In high school, I spent years sending cold emails to coaches, scrambling to put together materials, and hoping someone would notice.

After going through it myself, I built Athletefolio to give the next generation of players the professional presence they deserve. Every site I build is something I would have wanted when I was trying to get recruited out of high school.
